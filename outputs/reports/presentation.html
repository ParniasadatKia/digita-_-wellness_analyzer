The Diagnosis

Before tuning anything, we asked: can this even be learned?

0.16
the largest Cohen's d effect size between "Moderate" and "Severe" users, across every feature in the dataset — statistically, they don't differ.

A 4-class model (None/Mild/Moderate/Severe) scored only 49% accuracy. No amount of grid search fixes two labels that look identical in the data. So we merged Moderate + Severe into one data-justified class: High.
